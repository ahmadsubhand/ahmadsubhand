## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🎌 IngetAnime

A centralized anime platform to discover legal streaming sources, track watch progress, and check release schedules across platforms, reducing daily schedule-checking time by ~85% (from ~10 minutes to under 1 minute).

- **Frontend:** Next.js, React, TailwindCSS, Shadcn/UI
- **Backend:** NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL
- 🌐 [Repository](https://github.com/IngetAnime)
- 🚀 [Live Website](https://ingetanime.netlify.app)

### 🐄 TernakKu

A livestock management application that replaces error-prone manual records with centralized digital tracking of livestock inventory, genealogy, health records, and vaccination schedules to improve data accuracy and operational efficiency.

- **Mobile:** Dart, Flutter
- **Backend:** NestJS, Prisma, MySQL
- 📱 [Mobile App](https://github.com/TernakKu-IPB/ternakku-app)
- ⚙️ [Backend API](https://github.com/TernakKu-IPB/ternakku-backend)

### 🏛️ SPBE Portal

A government SPBE knowledge portal that centralizes documentation and addresses fragmented knowledge management through role-based article reviews and controlled publishing, ensuring unpublished content remains private while approved knowledge is accessible to the public.

- **Frontend:** Blade, Alpine.js, TailwindCSS
- **Backend:** Laravel, PHP
- 🌐 [Repository](https://github.com/ahmadsubhand/portal-spbe)
- 🚀 [Live Website](https://spbe.cibest.id)

### 🌾 Satu Data Pertanian Portal

A centralized agricultural data portal that makes fragmented agricultural statistics, geospatial data, and publications easier to discover and access for research and data-driven decision-making.

- **Frontend:** Inertia.js, React, TailwindCSS, Ant Design
- **Backend:** Laravel, PHP
- 🌐 [Repository](https://github.com/ahmadsubhand/satu-data-pertanian)
- 🚀 [Live Website](https://sdp.pertanian.go.id/)

### 🏅 MATASAPI

A livestock certification system that centralizes scattered documents to simplify document retrieval, automates notifications to reduce manual coordination, and digitizes certification workflows to reduce administrative time and operational costs.

- **Frontend:** Bootstrap
- **Backend:** Yii2, PHP, MySQL
- 🌐 [Repository](https://github.com/ahmadsubhand/sertifikasi-saspri)
- 🚀 [Live Website](https://sertifikasi.digdaya.net)
